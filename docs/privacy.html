Privacy Policy — Workout Importer

Last updated:

Workout Importer is published by Bota Trabalho ("we", "us"). This policy explains what data the app accesses, why, and how it's handled.

What the app accesses

  • Workout files you import. A workout file you choose (or the bundled sample) is read on your device to build the workout shown in the app. It is not uploaded anywhere except to the destination you explicitly choose to send it to (Garmin Connect or Apple Watch, below).
  • Garmin account access. If you choose to connect Garmin, the app uses Garmin's own sign-in page (OAuth) to ask for your permission to push workouts to your Garmin Connect account. We never see or store your Garmin username or password — only an access token Garmin issues after you approve, which is stored securely on your device (Keychain on iOS, encrypted storage on Android) and used solely to send workouts you choose to send. You can disconnect at any time in the app, which deletes this token from your device.
  • Apple Watch / HealthKit access. If you choose to send a workout to Apple Watch, the app uses Apple's WorkoutKit, which requires HealthKit authorization to schedule the workout on your paired watch. This happens entirely through Apple's system frameworks; workout data is handled by Apple's APIs, not sent to us.

What we don't do

  • We don't operate our own server that stores your workouts or account data.
  • We don't sell or share your data with third parties.
  • We don't use analytics or advertising SDKs that track you.

Third-party services

Connecting Garmin is subject to Garmin's own privacy policy. Using Apple Watch features is subject to Apple's HealthKit and WorkoutKit terms.
