Workout Importer

Workout Importer is a mobile app (iOS and Android) that imports structured running workouts — warmups, intervals, repeats, pace targets, cooldowns — and sends them to your Garmin device or Apple Watch so they're ready to run on your wrist, without manually re-entering each step.

It connects to your own Garmin Connect account (via Garmin's official sign-in) or Apple Watch (via Apple's WorkoutKit) to deliver the workout directly to your device.
